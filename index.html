Industries We Serve

From deep underwater agricultural borewells to extreme-temperature arc welding and heavy industrial utilities, Rialto cables power critical infrastructure worldwide.

01

Submersible & Agriculture

Specialized PVC & Rubber flat cables engineered for continuous submersion inside deep borewells up to 500 meters.

02

Industrial Arc Welding

High-conductivity Class 6 flexible copper welding cables with EPR/NBR sheath resistant to spatter, oil, and continuous flexing.

03

Power Utilities & XLPE

Steel wire and strip armoured XLPE power cables engineered for direct underground burial and heavy utility networks.

04

Renewable Solar Energy

EN 50618 & TÜV certified H1Z2Z2-K solar DC cables engineered for 1500V rooftop and utility-scale solar photovoltaic farms.

Battery & Inverter Banks

Ultra-fine copper stranding cables for EV automotive, industrial UPS battery banks, and solar power inverter connections.

06

Ports, Cranes & Machinery

Harmonized European HO7RN-F heavy-duty rubber cables resistant to grease, ozone, dragging, and extreme outdoor weather.

07

Commercial Infrastructure

Flame retardant low smoke (FRLS) PVC building wires and flexible control cables for commercial high-rises and data centres.

08

Global Export Markets

Cables manufactured, inspected by third-party agencies (SGS, TUV), and shipped in treated wooden reels to 50+ countries worldwide.

Quality & Design Governance

Indian Engineering at the Centre

All Rialto cable designs are developed, reviewed, and approved by our veteran in-house engineering team. Our engineering scope includes:

  • Cable design, conductor stranding, and insulation compound definition
  • Standards and specification compliance (IS, IEC, EN, BS, UL, AWG)
  • Subsea immersion, thermal aging, and mechanical flex assessment
  • Comprehensive QA inspection documentation and test certificates
  • Ongoing technical project support for global EPC contractors

Engineering accountability remains with Rialto Cables from initial design through to final containerized port delivery.

Core Advantages

Why Rialto Cables

Engineering-Led, Not Trade-Led

Every Rialto cable is designed and approved by our technical engineering team before it enters production. We control the metallurgy, compounding, quality system, and the manufacturing process — not just the purchase order.

Vertically Integrated Manufacturing

Rialto maintains complete in-house wire drawing, bunching, extrusion, and vulcanizing lines at our Vatva GIDC production facility. This is not subcontracted manufacturing — it is Rialto-controlled, owned production capacity.

Built for the Hardest Jobs

From underground water wells at 500 meters depth to heavy arc welding and outdoor solar installations, our cables are engineered for extreme mechanical abrasion, water immersion, chemical exposure, and continuous flexing.

Our Specialisation

Where We Lead

Submersible & Welding — Core Expertise

Rialto specializes in submersible pump cables and extra-flexible arc welding cables designed for extreme environmental and mechanical conditions, including agricultural borewells, municipal water lines, and arc welding equipment.

Our cables are engineered with pure Class 5 & 6 copper stranding and custom-formulated PVC/Rubber sheaths for long-term abrasion, water impermeability, and electrical safety.
